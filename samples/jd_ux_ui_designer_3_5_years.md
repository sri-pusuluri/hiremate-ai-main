## About Zool
At **Zool**, we engineer cutting-edge digital platforms, enterprise SaaS applications, and modern web experiences that blend intuitive simplicity with high-impact functionality. Our design culture is centered around empathy, precision, user-centric research, and pixel-perfect execution. We are looking for an ambitious, creative, and detail-driven **UX/UI Designer (3–5 Years Experience)** to shape our next generation of digital products.

---

## Role Overview
As a UX/UI Designer at Zool, you will be the bridge between complex user needs, business objectives, and elegant visual interfaces. You will own the design journey from early discovery and wireframing to high-fidelity prototyping, design system governance, and seamless developer handoff. You will collaborate closely with product managers, frontend engineers, and executive stakeholders to create engaging web and mobile experiences.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. User Research & Information Architecture
- Conduct quantitative and qualitative user research, user interviews, competitive bench-marking, and persona creation.
- Define information architecture, user journeys, task flows, sitemaps, and low-fidelity wireframes to validate conceptual direction early.
- Identify friction points in existing user funnels and propose data-informed design solutions.

### 2. UI Design & Design Systems
- Craft clean, modern, aesthetically stunning visual user interfaces for responsive web applications, mobile platforms (iOS/Android), and SaaS dashboards.
- Build, scale, and maintain Zool’s comprehensive Design System (tokens, atomic UI components, typography, responsive grids, and micro-interaction states) in **Figma**.
- Ensure accessibility standards (**WCAG 2.1 AA**) across contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, and touch targets.

### 3. Interactive Prototyping & Usability Testing
- Develop interactive, clickable, high-fidelity prototypes to communicate complex user interactions and animations.
- Plan and execute usability testing sessions, synthesize participant feedback, and iterate rapidly based on evidence.
- Present design rationales, research insights, and user stories confidently to cross-functional teams and leadership.

### 4. Engineering Collaboration & Developer Handoff
- Deliver structured, developer-ready design specifications in Figma (auto-layout, components with variants, design tokens, responsive constraints).
- Collaborate with frontend engineers (React, Angular, Tailwind CSS) during sprint cycles to ensure design fidelity during implementation.
- Conduct Design QA reviews before production releases to ensure visual consistency and animation smoothness.

---

## Qualifications & Requirements

### Must-Haves (Required):
- **Experience:** 3 to 5 years of proven experience as a UI/UX Designer, Product Designer, or Interaction Designer working on web applications and SaaS platforms.
- **Portfolio:** A strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end design case studies, wireframes, user journeys, design systems, and final production interfaces.
- **Tooling Mastery:** Expert-level proficiency in **Figma** (Auto-layout, Components, Variants, Interactive Components, Design Tokens).
- **Design Process:** Deep understanding of user-centered design methodologies, UX heuristics, and information architecture.
- **Technical Literacy:** Good understanding of web technologies (HTML5, CSS3/Tailwind CSS, responsive breakpoints, web performance constraints) so that designs are feasible to engineer.
- **Communication:** Outstanding written and verbal communication skills; ability to articulate the "why" behind design decisions.

### Nice-to-Haves (Bonus):
- Familiarity with design and prototyping tools like Framer, Principle, ProtoPie, or Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, After Effects).
- Experience designing complex SaaS workflows, analytics dashboards, or data-dense tables.
- Experience with A/B testing platforms, Google Analytics, or Hotjar/FullStory.
- Basic knowledge of CSS animations and SVG vector manipulation.
