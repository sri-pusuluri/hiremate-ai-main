# Job Description: Digital Marketing Specialist

## Role Overview
We are seeking an analytical and creative Digital Marketing Specialist to lead our online growth campaigns. You will own social media marketing, SEO/SEM performance, email marketing campaigns, and paid search budgets to drive conversion rates.

## Responsibilities
- Plan, execute, and monitor paid ads across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn.
- Research keywords and optimize blog posts/website structure for SEO.
- Write copy for email marketing newsletters and automated workflows.
- Monitor marketing dashboards using Google Analytics 4 and HubSpot.
- Collaborate with designers to build landing pages that convert traffic.

## Requirements
- 2+ years of experience in digital marketing or growth hacking.
- Proficiency with Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, and Google Analytics.
- Basic understanding of SEO concepts and tools (Ahrefs, Semrush).
- Excellent copy-writing skills and marketing intuition.
